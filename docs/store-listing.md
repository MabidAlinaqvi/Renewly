# Google Play listing: Renewly

**App name (30 max):** Renewly: Subscription Tracker

**Short description (80 max):**
Track subscriptions, see your monthly spend, get reminded before every renewal.

**Full description:**

Stop paying for subscriptions you forgot about.

Renewly keeps every subscription in one list and tells you before money leaves your account: Netflix, Spotify, ChatGPT, iCloud, your gym, your phone plan, anything you pay for regularly.

WHAT YOU GET
• Your total monthly and yearly spend at a glance
• Every subscription sorted by its next renewal
• Reminders on the day, or 1, 2, 3 or 7 days before
• Free-trial alerts before a trial turns into a charge
• Quick add for popular services, with links to their cancel pages
• Weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly billing
• Over 30 currencies, all added up in your currency with daily exchange rates
• A home-screen widget with your total and next renewals
• Charts: where your money goes and what the next 6 months will cost
• Travelling? Renewly offers to switch to the local currency
• Backup and restore to Drive, email or any file

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your subscriptions stay on your phone.

## Data safety form

- Data collected: **None**

- Data is processed only on the device. Backups are files the user chooses where to send.
- Approximate location: requested only on phones without a SIM, used once to pick the default currency, never stored. It's processed on the device (Android's geocoder), so Google's guidance treats it as not collected.
