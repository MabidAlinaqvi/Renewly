Renewly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Renewly is a subscription tracker made by Independent Studio. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Renewly has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. The subscriptions, prices, dates and notes you enter are stored only on your phone.

Default currency and location

On first start, Renewly picks your currency from the country of your mobile network or SIM card. This happens on your phone and needs no permission.

Only on a phone with no SIM and no mobile network, Renewly asks for approximate location once. Android's built-in geocoder, provided by Google, turns it into a country. Renewly keeps only the currency it picked and never stores or sends your location. You can refuse, and change the currency any time in Settings.

Notifications

Renewly asks permission to show notifications so it can remind you before renewals. Reminders are scheduled on your phone and never sent through any server.

Backups

When you choose Back up, Renewly creates a file and opens your phone's share menu. The file goes only where you send it, such as Google Drive or email. Restoring reads a file you pick.

Links

Cancel and manage links open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Renewly does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Renewly, or clearing its storage, deletes everything it stored.
